Rabat.otf — licence and provenance
==================================

Rabat ("Font Maghribi Mabsout RABAT v1.0") by Youssef JABRI.
Source: https://sourceforge.net/projects/maghribifonts/

Shipped UNMODIFIED, byte-for-byte as downloaded. Its licence, as stated
in the font's own name table, is:

  Copyright (c) 2010 by Youssef JABRI. All rights reserved. May be used
  Freely. No copying of glyphs or reverse engeneering allowed without the
  written permission of the copyright owner. yjabri@ensa.ump.ma

Because of that clause, the honorific Presets glyphs are NOT merged into
this font, so the Presets buttons render as missing-glyph boxes in it.
Do not run scripts/mergePuaGlyphs.py on this file without the author's
written permission. See .claude/rules/bundled-fonts.md.

Caveat: a design exported as SVG or PDF embeds this font's glyph outlines
in the exported file, which the "no copying of glyphs" clause may reach.
Raster exports (PNG/JPEG) carry pixels only.
