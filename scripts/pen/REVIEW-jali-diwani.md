# Jali Diwani stroke review

The approval ledger for the Jali Diwani forms, as `REVIEW.md` is for
Thuluth's: every form, when it was drafted, what it was drawn from, and the
user's verdict. A form is approved only when the user says so. Under the
user's "within reason" rule (2026-10-20, as for Thuluth) a form matched to
its reference within reason is recorded here before the user has looked,
marked as awaiting the user's look. References are research material: the
app credits no author, and no plate outline is copied into the library.

How each stroke is drawn, and why, is in
`docs/calligraphy/jali-diwani-ledger.md`. The forms are not in the app's
library yet: where they sit beside Thuluth's in `penLibrary` is still to be
agreed with the user.
